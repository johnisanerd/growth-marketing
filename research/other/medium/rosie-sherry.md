## 7. The Guide to Community Discovery
**Author:** Rosie Sherry
**Date:** Mon, 27 Jan 2025 19:45:04 GMT
**URL:** https://rosiesherry.medium.com/the-guide-to-community-discovery-24f024783199?source=rss-cbb112f6f374------2
**Tags:** community, research-community, community-discovery, community-building
The Guide to Community Discovery is a comprehensive guide to community research with strategies and tactics to learn about and with your people. Ultimately, the goal is to build a better (community) business. At the heart of Community Discovery is the focus on research to benefit the whole business. In essence, it is living, breathing, active, and continuous community research. I’ve been building community for 20 years. As I was trying to explain how I approached building community I felt that the approaches that existed did not align with how I’ve built community. To build community we should start with Community Discovery. Once we know about it, we will then realise it is an ongoing effort that never ends as building community is continuous, not static. Community still feels mysterious to many and a big part of this is down to a lack of understanding of our ecosystems and how everything is interconnected. Community Discovery is about trying to make sense of all the nodes so that we can make better decisions and build better products. Community work is pure gold when we understand what we need to look for. A mindset of Community Discovery helps us look for what matters in ways that can give value and bring impact to the wider business. Gone should be the days where community works in silos. Community Discovery can empower the whole business with powerful community knowledge. The ideas within this guide are things that I actively practice. To me, this is not theory. This is the real work of building community, there is real method to my madness! Buy The Guide to Community Discovery or visit Rosieland for more community building goodness.
