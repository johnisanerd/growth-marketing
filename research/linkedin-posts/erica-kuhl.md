## Post 1 — 2019-12-19
**Title:** Why Ask Why?
**Type:** Article
**Likes:** 74
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-ask-erica-kuhl
**Content:**

My daughter just reminded me that we’re closing out a whole decade as we ring in 2020. Somehow that fact escaped me. I don’t know about you but 2019 was a BIG year of change for me. I have heard this from many people as if change was physically in the air. Maybe it’s something about the number 19. Maybe it’s about wanting to make a leap of faith before a brand new decade is upon us. Maybe it’s strictly coincidence. Either way 2019 brought change.I am inspired by so much as I close out this year, but one thing that struck me most was a concept that Craig Forman from Culture Amp introduced to me. Starting with Why. He pointed me to a fabulous Ted Talk by Simon Sinek to explain this concept. We had been in deep conversation about company culture, what it means, and why some stand out. We also talked why people chose to do business with one particular person over another. We also talked about what creates the most passionate and engaged communities. Suffice it to say we were talking for hours! It all boils down to the simple concept of Starting with Why and not What. According to Simon Sinek “the goal is to do business with people who believe what you believe”. And “people don’t buy what you do they buy why you do it and what you do serves as the proof of what you believe“I love this concept so much I’m going to make it my 2020 goal to reframe and own this innovative way of thinking. I definitely led with "What" when I was building out the Trailblazer Community at Salesforce and trying to show value, ask for money and resources. I’d say: "I’m building a one-stop-shop for Salesforce customers to get their questions answered, share ideas and connect. A place for customers, partners and developers to learn, connect, have fun and give back. I need x$ and x resources to do this, will you support me?" If I reframe it to Simon Sinek’s way I’d say: "I’m creating a way to change peoples lives through building authentic connections where everyone can succeed together. I do this by creating a one-stop-shop community where customers, partners, and developers can learn, connect, have fun and give back. I need x$ and x resources to do this, will you support me?"I can only imagine the looks I’d get. “People don’t buy what you do they buy why you do it.” In my current business as a solo consultant I often get asked what I do. Or a potential client asks me to describe what I do so they can decide whether to work with me or not. Now I’m going to lead with “Why”. I’m going to say: “I believe that building meaningful connections with your customers will transform the way you do business. I’ll help you do that by providing the formula to unlock the power of your customers through community building.”This is going to take practice and refinement but I’m excited to kick off the new year with this exciting new challenge. How about you give it a go....what’s your why?“When the why is clear, the how is easy.”
